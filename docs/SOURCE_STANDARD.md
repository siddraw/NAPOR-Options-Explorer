SOURCE STANDARD (non-negotiable)
ALLOWED as a citation: AP, Reuters, AFP, BBC, NPR, PBS, NYT, WSJ, Washington Post, Bloomberg, Financial Times, CBS, ABC, NBC, CNN, Al Jazeera, Axios, Politico, Times of Israel / Haaretz (note they are Israeli outlets), USNI News, Defense News, Breaking Defense, The War Zone, Stars and Stripes, official statements (CENTCOM, DoD, White House, Treasury/OFAC, UKMTO, UN, IAEA).
OFFICIAL OR PRIMARY SOURCES (approved 2026-10-29) can be the citation, and they score like an allowed outlet (0.80 when read in full): government and military sites (.gov, .mil); legislative records (Congressional Record, govinfo.gov, congress.gov, CRS reports at crsreports.congress.gov); courts and the Justice Department; the UN and other international bodies (IAEA, KEDO, OAS, NATO); a foreign government's own ministry, but only for what that government itself did or said (for example Japan's defense ministry); and official archives such as presidential libraries and archived White House sites. Use them for what the body did, said or decided; they are not proof of a claim they make about someone else. A website that only hosts a copy of an official document (everycrsreport.com, GlobalSecurity.org, iranwatch.org, the American Presidency Project) is a mirror, not the official source: label it "CRS report (via everycrsreport.com)" and it keeps the score of an outlet that is not on the list (0.60) unless the same document is open at its official URL. sgp.fas.org is not official.
LABEL RULE (approved 2026-10-29): label and link only the source you got the event from. The outlet label must name the site the URL points to (the page that was read). A wire or outlet story read on another site is labelled honestly, for example "AP (via kpax.com)" or "Reuters (via lieu.house.gov)", and the URL stays the page that was read. Never put the original outlet's name on a copy without "via". tests/test_sources.py fails on a mismatch.
Iranian state media (IRNA, Tasnim, Press TV) is allowed ONLY to document what Iran CLAIMS, and must be labelled as a claim.
NOT ALLOWED as a citation: Wikipedia (any language), blogs, Substack, aggregators, regional content farms, tabloids (Daily Mirror, Daily Mail, Express, Sun), think-tank opinion pieces used for facts, social media.
Wikipedia may be used ONLY as a map to find the footnoted articles; the claim must then be re-confirmed in the cited outlet itself, and only the outlet is cited.
Never invent a URL or a quote: only report URLs that came from your search results or fetches. Mark each source seen="full text" if you read the article, or seen="snippet" if you only saw the search-result headline/snippet.
LINK RULE (approved 2026-10-28): an event counts only if it has at least one source with a working https URL from an allowed outlet or an official or primary source. A report that only names an outlet, with no link, gets zero credit. An event with no linked source is excluded from every count, rate, backtest and recount, in all five theaters.
If you cannot find a credible outlet for a claim, the verdict is not_confirmed. Do not fill the gap with a weaker source.
