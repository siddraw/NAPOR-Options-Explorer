# Next tabs: conflicts and standoffs the US is in (research memo, 2026-10-22)

**Status (2026-10-29):** Houthis, North Korea, China and Venezuela / Caribbean are built (tabs on the page), with logs reaching back to 2014, 1993, 1995 and 1999. The others are still research only.

Research only, no code. Sources are allowed outlets read in this session (URLs from search or fetch
results only). Case counts are **rough estimates from what the coverage suggests, not counts**; each
must be built and checked like the Iran log before any number is shown. Uncertain points are marked.

What makes a good NAPOR tab: the US makes repeated, datable moves (strikes, sanctions, deployments,
talks) and an adversary answers in datable ways, both covered by allowed outlets, so "x of n past
cases" has a real n.

## 1. Houthis / Red Sea (Yemen)
**Status.** As of Oct 13, 2026 the active fighting is Saudi-Houthi: the Saudi-led coalition struck oil
sites in western Yemen, and Houthi missiles and drones hit Saudi airports (at least 20 killed in a week).
The Houthis threatened "a swift and painful response targeting the U.S." if the US joins; Trump said
"We may. We're gonna look at it." CBS reports no US strikes on the Houthis in 2026; the last US campaign
was March-May 2025. Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping are not reported this month. ([CBS, Oct 13](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-saudi-arabia-houthi/))
- **US options:** hold; diplomacy (via Oman/Saudi); sanctions/terror designations; interdiction of Iranian
  arms shipments; strikes on launch sites; strikes on leaders; naval escort/posture.
- **Cases 2019-2026 (rough):** large. The Nov 2023 - May 2025 Red Sea crisis alone holds dozens of US
  strikes and a few hundred Houthi attacks, mostly covered by CENTCOM, AP and USNI. Much of it is
  continuous fighting, so the continuous-war rule will remove many triggers; a few dozen countable cases is
  plausible (uncertain).
- **Data difficulty:** medium. Dense, well-sourced record; hard part is separating replies from routine
  daily attacks, and Houthi claims (al-Masirah) must be claims only.
- **Fit:** strong. Overlaps the Iran engine (Iran-backed group, same sources, R-proxy logic).

## 2. North Korea
**Status.** North Korea fired multiple ballistic missiles on Oct 12, 2026, one flying more than 900 km on
an "irregular" path, after an Oct 3 hypersonic test; Kim has rejected Trump's calls to revive talks.
([AP via News4Jax, Oct 12](https://www.news4jax.com/news/world/2026/10/12/south-koreas-military-says-north-korea-fired-a-projectile-toward-the-sea/))
- **US options:** hold; talks/summit offer; sanctions; joint drills with South Korea/Japan; strategic-asset
  deployments (bombers, carriers, submarine visits); missile-defense moves.
- **Cases 2019-2026 (rough):** many dozens of US/allied drills, deployments and sanctions, and a long list of
  missile tests that often follow them within days. Replies are mostly tests and statements, not attacks.
- **Data difficulty:** low-medium. Launches are dated by South Korea's JCS and Japan; drills by USFK/INDOPACOM.
  Risk categories would need re-wording (escalation = test or provocation, not strikes).
- **Fit:** good for the method; different harm profile (few casualties).

## 3. China: Taiwan Strait and South China Sea
**Status.** On Oct 20, 2026 China sent naval forces to shadow USS Charleston near Sabina Shoal (Spratlys),
saying it "illegally intruded"; in a separate standoff a Chinese coast guard ship entered restricted
waters near Taiwan's Pratas Islands. ([Reuters via TimesLIVE, Oct 20](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/world/2026-10-20-china-sends-naval-forces-to-monitor-us-warship-near-disputed-shoal/))
- **US options:** hold; transits/FONOPs; arms sales to Taiwan; sanctions/export controls; high-level visits;
  talks; deployments.
- **Cases 2019-2026 (rough):** dozens of Taiwan Strait transits, arms-sale announcements and visits, with
  datable PLA replies (large drills after the 2022 Pelosi visit and 2023-2024 events). Most replies are
  non-kinetic.
- **Data difficulty:** medium. Good coverage; PLA "routine" activity makes it hard to tell a reply from
  background; Chinese state media only as claims.
- **Fit:** good; needs new categories (drills, incursions) rather than strikes.

## 4. Caribbean / Eastern Pacific boat strikes and Venezuela ("Operation Southern Spear")
**Status.** US forces captured Nicolás Maduro in Caracas on Jan 2-3, 2026, after months of strikes on
alleged drug boats ([AP via PBS, Jan 3](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/how-u-s-forces-captured-venezuelan-leader-nicolas-maduro-in-caracas)).
The boat strikes continue: an Oct 4, 2026 strike killed four, bringing the toll to at least 235 deaths in
70 strikes since September 2025, with no evidence offered that the boats carried drugs ([Al Jazeera, Oct 5](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/5/latest-us-military-strike-on-alleged-drug-boat-kills-four-in-caribbean)).
Venezuela's current politics (interim leadership under Delcy Rodríguez, US presence) were not confirmed in an
allowed outlet this session: **uncertain**.
- **US options:** hold; boat strikes; land strikes; sanctions; naval posture; talks with the interim government.
- **Cases 2019-2026 (rough):** about 70 US strikes, but few datable "replies" by an adversary (traffickers do
  not answer in public), so "reply" rates would be thin. Harm (deaths per strike) is well reported.
- **Data difficulty:** medium-high for the reply side; low for the US side.
- **Fit:** partial. Works for harm and political categories, weak for escalation.

## 5. Mexico cartels
**Status.** About 500 US troops with helicopters and drones went to the border after an alleged late-September
shooting at US Coast Guard members from the Mexican side; Mexico says it detected no shots and asked for evidence.
No US strikes inside Mexico are reported. ([Al Jazeera, Oct 5](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/5/mexico-seeks-evidence-of-border-shooting-that-prompted-us-troop-deployment))
- **US options:** hold; sanctions/terror designations; border deployments; strikes (threatened, not used);
  cooperation with Mexico.
- **Cases (rough):** few US military moves; many sanctions. Adversary replies hard to attribute.
- **Data difficulty:** high. **Fit:** weak until there are datable US actions and responses.

## 6. Russia-Ukraine
**Status.** The US is a mediator, not a combatant: Witkoff met Putin in St Petersburg on Oct 21, 2026, and both
Putin and Zelenskyy back three-way talks, possibly in Abu Dhabi in November; fighting and long-range strikes
continue; the US temporarily lifted sanctions on Russian diesel sales as leverage. ([Al Jazeera, Oct 21](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/21/witkoff-in-russia-for-putin-meeting-after-positive-talks-with-zelenskyy))
- **US options:** hold; talks; sanctions up or down; weapons/intelligence aid to Ukraine.
- **Cases (rough):** many US aid and sanctions moves since 2022, but Russia's "replies" are hard to separate from
  the war's own tempo. **Data difficulty:** high. **Fit:** weak for action-reaction; better as a negotiation tracker.

## 7. Gaza
**Status.** A year after the US-brokered ceasefire: hostages returned and major combat ended, but Hamas has not
disarmed, Israel holds more than half of Gaza, the stabilization force has not materialized, and Israeli strikes
have killed over 1,400 Palestinians since the ceasefire (Gaza Health Ministry). ([AP via Inquirer, Oct 10](https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/gaza-ceasefire-anniversary-more-dead-israeli-strikes-continue-rebuilding-hamas-trump-netanyahu-20261010.html))
- **US role:** broker (Board of Peace), not a party to the fighting. **Fit:** weak for US options; skip.
