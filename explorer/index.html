The options the US has right now in the Iran war, and what followed the last times the US did something like each one, in percentages with the margin of error shown. It lays out options and their risks. It does not rank them or say which to pick.

Percentages are x of n past cases in the NAPOR log. The bar shows the 95% range the true rate could plausibly sit in (Wilson interval). With 2 to 7 cases that range is very wide, so read each number as a hint. "After" is not "because of". Oil prices, strain with allies and harm to ordinary Iranians are not measured.

The risk index averages five measured rates (equal weights unless you change them). It summarizes how past cases of that kind went. It is not a forecast and not a recommendation, and the options are not ranked.

War intensity counts logged hostile events in the 14 days before each date (2 points per direct strike, 1 per shipping attack or blockade move, +2 if someone died). Big campaigns are logged as a few rows, so hot weeks are understated.

This page reads data.json, which the weekly NAPOR agent regenerates every Monday. "Right now" notes are the agent's reading of the news, not data.
