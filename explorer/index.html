Percentages are x of n past cases in the NAPOR log, with the 95% range the true rate could plausibly sit in. Small samples give wide ranges, so read each number as a hint. "After" is not "because of".

The risk index summarizes how past cases of that kind went. It is not a forecast and not a recommendation, and the options are not ranked. See "How this works".

War intensity counts logged hostile events in the 14 days before each date (2 points per direct strike, 1 per shipping attack or blockade move, +2 if someone died). Big campaigns are logged as a few rows, so hot weeks are understated.

The weekly NAPOR agent regenerates the data every Monday. "Right now" notes are the agent's reading of the news, not data.
