NAPOR Options Explorer

Each option the US has in the Iran war, and what followed the last times the US did something like it, in percentages with the margin of error shown. It lays out the options and their risks. It does not rank them or say which to pick.

Percentages are x of n past cases in the NAPOR log. The bar shows the 95% range the true rate could plausibly sit in (Wilson interval). With 2 to 5 cases that range is very wide, so read each number as a hint. "After" is not "because of". Oil prices, strain with allies and harm to ordinary Iranians are not measured.

War intensity counts logged hostile events in the 14 days before each date (2 points per direct strike, 1 per shipping attack or blockade move, +2 if someone died). Big campaigns are logged as a few rows, so hot weeks are understated.

This page reads data.json, which the weekly NAPOR agent regenerates every Monday.
